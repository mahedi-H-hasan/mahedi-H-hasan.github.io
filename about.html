Biography

I'm a Lecturer in the Dept. of Computer Science & Engineering at IUBAT (International University of Business Agriculture and Technology), Dhaka, where I've taught since July 2023. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from CUET (Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology), graduating with a CGPA of 3.85/4.00 and ranking 5th of 120 students in my cohort.

My research spans human-centric computer vision, spatial-temporal video analytics, multi-modal scene understanding, vision-language models, human-in-the-loop machine learning, medical image analysis & biometrics, and intelligent transportation systems. Alongside teaching, I supervise undergraduate thesis teams and coach competitive programming at the IUBAT IT Society. In 2026 I received the Miyan Research Institute Award for Faculty Excellence for six peer-reviewed publications within two years of joining as faculty.
