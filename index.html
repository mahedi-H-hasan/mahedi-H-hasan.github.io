About

I'm a Lecturer in the Dept. of Computer Science & Engineering at IUBAT (International University of Business Agriculture and Technology), Dhaka, and a B.Sc. graduate in CSE from CUET (Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology), where I ranked 5th of 120 students (CGPA 3.85/4.00).

My research spans human-centric computer vision, spatial-temporal video analytics, multi-modal scene understanding, vision-language models, human-in-the-loop machine learning, medical image analysis & biometrics, and intelligent transportation systems. Alongside teaching, I supervise undergraduate thesis teams and coach competitive programming at IUBAT IT Society, and in 2026 received the Miyan Research Institute Award for Faculty Excellence for six peer-reviewed journal publications within two years of joining as faculty.
